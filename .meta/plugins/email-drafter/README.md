# Plugin: Email Drafter

**Pattern**: Prompts-only
**Invocation type**: On-demand
**Status**: Starter — the tone and sign-off style are the most team-specific parts; update those in the prompt

---

## What It Does

Drafts a professional email from bullet points or rough context. Handles the most common consulting email types: status updates, follow-ups, ask/escalation, and meeting requests.

## When to Use

- When you know what you want to say but not how to say it professionally
- Client-facing emails where tone matters
- Follow-up emails after a meeting

## Note

This is intentionally minimal — email style varies significantly by sender, relationship, and org. The starter prompt gives you a clean professional output; refine the tone instructions for your specific voice.
